Support

Three tasks a day. No more.

Most questions about Sense are really questions about the rule it is built on. This page answers those first, then the practical ones.

Something wrong, or something missing? Write to CONTACT@EXAMPLE.COM. Say which version of Sense and which version of macOS you are on, and what you expected to happen.

How Sense works

Why only three?

Because the limit is the point. A list that can hold everything tells you nothing about what today is for. Three is not a default or a suggestion, and it is not a setting. Once your three are done you may take on a bonus task, up to three of them, one at a time, and those sit outside the promise: they never roll over and never move your streak.

Why can I not swap a task after I set the day?

Setting the day is the promise. If you could quietly replace a hard task with an easy one at four in the afternoon, the promise would mean nothing. After you set the day you can tick a task off, undo that, change its note, and run its clock. You cannot add, remove or swap.

What happens to a task I do not finish?

At midnight it rolls over to the next day and takes one of tomorrow's three slots. That is the whole mechanism: unfinished work costs a slot rather than sliding down a list. If three tasks roll over, tomorrow is already full.

I am stuck with a task I will never do.

At the day boundary you can give up a rolled-over task once. It goes away, it does not count as finished, and Sense records that it happened. That is the only exit, and it is meant to feel like a decision.

Why does a day I skipped count as missed?

A day you never set is a day you never promised anything. The tasks you had picked go back to their backlogs and the day counts as missed. Anything that had rolled over still rolls on.

Practical questions

Where is my data?

In Sense's own folder on your Mac. Nothing is stored online and nothing is synced. See the privacy policy for the detail.

How do I move to another Mac?

Settings has an export that writes all of Sense to one backup file. Copy that file across and import it on the other Mac. It restores your projects, tasks, history and settings.

The keyboard shortcut does not open a new task.

Command Shift N works while any app is in front, as long as Sense is running. If another app has claimed the same combination, that app wins, because macOS gives the shortcut to whoever asked first.

The morning reminder does not arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Sense in System Settings under Notifications. Settings in Sense has a button that takes you straight there. The reminder only fires on days you have not set yet.

Sense does not start when I log in.

Turn the switch in Settings off and on again. macOS keeps that setting per app, and it can go stale after the app has moved to a different folder.

I see no suggestions for how long a task takes.

Those come from Apple Intelligence and only appear on Macs that support it and have it switched on. Everywhere a suggestion could appear works the same without one, so nothing is missing from the app itself.

Can I change the language?

Sense speaks English, Dutch, Polish, French and Spanish. It follows your Mac by default, and Settings holds a chooser that changes it while the app stays open. What you typed yourself never gets translated.

The month report is empty.

The report reads from days you actually set. A month in which you never set a day has nothing to show. Everything in it is worked out again from your history each time you open it, so it always matches what really happened.
