Sense

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Sense collects nothing about you. There is no account, no server, and no network connection of any kind in the app.

The short version. Everything you type into Sense stays in a file on your own Mac. It is never sent anywhere, because Sense has no way to send it. Nobody, including the developer, can see it.

What Sense stores, and where

Sense keeps the tasks you write, the notes you add to them, the names and colours of your projects, which three tasks you picked on which day, what you finished, and how long a task took while its clock was running.

All of it lives inside the app's own folder on your Mac, in your Library. Sense reads and writes nothing outside that folder, apart from the one file you point at yourself when you export a backup or a monthly PDF.

What Sense does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or profile.
  • No cloud storage and no syncing between devices.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party services, frameworks, or trackers of any kind.
  • No network requests at all. Sense runs inside Apple's App Sandbox without permission to use the network, and contains no networking code.

Suggestions

On Macs that support Apple Intelligence, Sense can suggest how long a task is likely to take and what kind of attention it needs. Those suggestions are produced by Apple's on-device model. The text of your task is handled on your Mac and is not sent to the developer or to any other company. On Macs without Apple Intelligence the feature is simply absent, and every screen works the same without it.

Notifications

The morning reminder and the note on the first of the month are local notifications, scheduled by the app on your own Mac. They do not travel over a network and no push service is involved.

Backups and exports

Exporting a backup or a monthly PDF writes a file to the location you choose in the save panel. From that moment the file is yours to look after. Sense does not upload it, copy it, or keep a second copy.

Children

Sense is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Removing your data

Deleting Sense removes its folder and everything in it. If you want to start over while keeping the app, Settings can reset it. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing left behind to request or erase.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you the right to see, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. Sense holds none, so those rights have nothing to act on here. Your own copy is always readable: the backup export writes all of it to a single file whenever you ask.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sense ever handles data differently, this page will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
