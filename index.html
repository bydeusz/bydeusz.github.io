Sense

Three tasks a day.
No more.

A to-do app for macOS

You pick three things in the morning and set the day. Whatever you leave unfinished at midnight takes one of tomorrow's three slots, so an unkept promise costs something real instead of sliding down a list.

Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no tracking, and no network connection of any kind.
